# Business analysis training pack

11 UTF-8 CSV files, 66 rows. All names, contacts, quantities and events are authored synthetic training data. All dates are explicit original synthetic dates. Money is BDT; fabric is kg; finished goods are pieces. Byte size and SHA-256 are recorded in manifest.json and checked by npm run data:verify:business. No Northwind/UCI original source file or attribution is changed.

Upload 01–03 and 05–08 as Operations in Operations namespace, in reference order. Finance uploads 04 and 09–10. A transactional CSV creates a Draft; use the UI's approval/business action afterwards. File 08 requires the focus Sales Order to be confirmed and the BOM approved. Review and commit each whole-file preview; repeated identical source IDs skip, while changed content is rejected.

The focus order SO-BA26-0001 is 10,000 T-shirts at BDT 450. Its isolated item/BOM needs 2,200 kg fabric. Post 1,400 kg opening stock, buy and accept the uncovered 800 kg at BDT 500/kg, match/post the supplier bill, reserve/issue, produce, release QC, dispatch, post the customer invoice and allocate payments. These later documents are created from their linked UI action with actual current business dates; they are not pre-posted CSV fixtures. No invoice, stock or salary ledger is inserted by this pack.

Operations requests BOM/Merchandising/PO reviews; another permitted account approves. Admin approves the three requests. Finance reviews attendance/components, submits payroll, and Admin approves. Finance creates/posts the actual BDT 2,000,000 customer receipt, BDT 400,000 supplier payment and BDT 29,000 salary payment using the current form date, never a date before the saved obligation. No fixed password is contained in the pack.

Focus-only expected selected-event changes: customer invoice BDT 4,500,000, ending AR BDT 2,500,000, AP zero, salary payable zero, raw/finished physical stock zero, and net cash movement BDT 1,571,000. Existing unrelated business balances remain in company totals; inspect this buyer/source/payment references or compare before and after. Net cash movement is not profit, full garment costing or a bank balance.

11-sales-pattern-inquiries.csv is optional: 48 Draft inquiries across 12 months and six buyers. They are recorded demand, not historical posted sales/revenue. For a compact analysis exercise, explicitly quote/confirm P041–P048 (September and October) and use 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-31 in Sales & Buyer Health. With the focus order confirmed, current order value is BDT 7,642,500 over 5 orders, previous equal-length period has BDT 3,920,000 over 4 orders, repeat buyers are 1 of 3, and two previous-period buyers have no current orders. These context orders need not be manufactured just to inspect the order book.

An invoice generated from delivery uses its actual issue date and a 30-day due date. An initially not-yet-due balance is correct; overdue amounts are not fabricated for chart appearance. Collection Priority permits an explicitly labelled as-of date for balance/aging inspection, not a prediction of future receipts.

Use the new pack IDs for the focused exercise; do not reset a populated DB or replay a different-content source under an existing ID. If an item already has this pack's stock movements, continue its related documents rather than posting opening stock again. This is one full-quantity, one-component BOM example, not multi-warehouse/lot/partial-quantity manufacturing or a scale/security benchmark. The full role-by-role guide is in BUSINESS_REPORTS_SETUP_BANGLA.md and the accompanying chat.
